The solar system

Saturn

Saturn is the sixth planet of the solar system. It is the only planet in the solar system with very visible rings. It is also a gas planet/jovian planet.

Earth

Earth is the third planet of the solar system. It is the only planet in the solar system which is habitable and supports life.

Venus

Venus is the second planet of the solar system. It is the hottest planet in the solar system and doesn't have any moons
